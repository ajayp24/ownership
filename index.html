SEC Ownership Intelligence
README

Who holds, who owns, who votes — straight from SEC filings

Learn how ownership of US public companies is reported, explore real SEC data for 14 companies, and turn your own filings into structured data. Everything runs in your browser — no server, no sign-in, nothing uploaded.

Application A

Ownership Intelligence Dashboard

Learn the terms and filing types, then see who holds and owns each company — with every figure linked to its SEC filing and date.

  • Step-by-step walkthrough of one company
  • 10 large and 4 small companies, real EDGAR data
  • Ownership map, network graph, history charts

Application B

SEC Filing Parser

Drop in a 13F, 13D, 13G or Form 3/4/5 (XML, HTML, TXT, CSV or ZIP) and get clean, validated data you can export or send to the dashboard.

  • Automatic filing-type detection
  • JSON viewer, validation, unmapped fields kept
  • Export JSON, Excel, CSV, ZIP · "Send to dashboard"
Open the parser →
Single file (~1 MB). Source version
Application C

UEIM — Entity & Investment Manager Master

One master of entities, roles and relationships built from SEC filings. See the same entity from every side: what BlackRock files, who reports rights over Apple's shares, what a manager manages, and how they all connect — every link traced to its filing.

  • Entity, Role, Relationship, Filing and Position masters
  • Filer, Ownership, Investment Manager and Network views
  • BlackRock and Apple explained, from BlackRock's 13G/A and its full 13F
